# 🐱 Personal cat food allergy checker

I created this personal project, because I found out that my cat has allergies to most ingredients found in dry food. 
I was in pursuit to find her new food that wasn't harmful to her. The problem was, I had to look at each bag of dry food at the store,
look at each ingredient in small text, and compare it to the list of allergies I have of hers. That process was time consuming and frankly, 
discouraging. So I decided to create a small code, that would automate that process for me.

---
## How it works
it takes an image of the ingredients list of the food you want to check.
turn the image into a list.
compare the ingredients to the allergies provided beforehand.
